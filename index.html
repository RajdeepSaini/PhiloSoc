WHAT IS PHILOSOPHY?

"The unexamined life is not worth living."

— Socrates · 399 BCE
PHILOSOC · THE PHILOSOPHY SOCIETY OF IIITD
FOUNDATIONAL MANIFESTO

A discipline that begins wherever certainty ends.

Philosophy is not a museum of historical dogmas. At IIIT Delhi, it is our intellectual laboratory — an uncompromising practice of interrogating the hidden premises behind artificial intelligence, ethical agency, truth, and consciousness.

01

Epistemology & Truth

In an era of generative models and synthetic consensus, how do we distinguish genuine cognition from mechanical mimicry?

02

Ethics & Agency

When algorithmic systems govern social architecture and high-stakes choices, where does human moral duty reside?

03

The Examined Self

To transcend pure engineering efficiency and ask what it actually means to lead a conscious, meaningful life.

ABOUT THE SOCIETY

The counterweight to computational velocity.

PhiloSoc is IIIT Delhi's philosophy society — a sanctuary for students, thinkers, and skeptics. In an institute driven by algorithmic efficiency and relentless optimization, we create the deliberate space to question premises, debate ethics, and dissect what it means to be human.

01

The Socratic Circles

Bi-weekly open floor disputations on artificial consciousness, free will, moral accountability, and institutional dogmas.

02

The Reading Symposia

Close textual examinations spanning ancient Stoicism and Indian schools to Sartre, Camus, Gödel, and post-human philosophy.

03

The Broadsheet & Dispatches

Original student essays, dialectic polemics, and interdisciplinary monographs published through the society press.

03 · THE COMMUNITY

WHY JOIN US?

The counterweight to computational velocity. At IIIT Delhi, we create the deliberate space to pause, question premises, and think together.

01

Open-Floor Socratic Circles

Bi-weekly disputations on artificial consciousness, free will, moral duty, and institutional dogmas — without taboo.

02

Zero Gatekeeping, Real Camaraderie

No prerequisites, no coding tests, no resumes. A genuine community of seniors and classmates who listen and converse.

03

The Literary Broadsheet & Salon

A platform to publish your original poetry, philosophical polemics, and essays — or simply ponder over chai.
